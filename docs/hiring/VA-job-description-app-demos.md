## What this is

We sell a custom rewards/loyalty app to local businesses (coffee shops, salons, medspas, restaurants, etc.). Our pitch works way better when the owner can see a **demo app already built for their business** — their logo, their colors, their rewards.

Your job is simple: **find local businesses and build a quick demo app for each one in our app builder.** No coding — it's a point-and-click tool. We'll train you and give you everything you need.

Then our sales rep walks in and shows the owner the finished demo. You make the demos; we close the deals.

---

## What you'll do

**1. Find businesses (California)**
- Use Google Maps / Google Search to find local businesses that would benefit from a loyalty app.
- Grab the basics: business name, logo, address, phone, and a good photo of the place (their Google/website photos are fine).

> **Why the research phase matters — read this carefully.** The research is the most important part; picking the wrong businesses wastes everyone's time. We're targeting **small, local, owner-run businesses** that genuinely look like they could use an app — a single coffee shop, a neighborhood salon, a family restaurant, a local medspa. We are **not** targeting big corporate chains. The quick tells that a business is **too big / skip it:** a huge Google review count (e.g. thousands of reviews), **multiple locations**, or a **slick, clearly expensive professional website** — those already have their own systems and a marketing team. The tells that a business is a **good target:** a modest review count, **one or two locations**, and a **simple, basic, or no website**. When in doubt, lean local and lean "this place could use the help."

**2. Build a demo app (5–10 min each in our builder)**
For each business, just:
- ✅ Add the **business logo**
- ✅ Set the **brand colors** (match their existing branding)
- ✅ Add **at least 4 rewards** (e.g. "Free coffee," "10% off," "Buy 9 get 1 free")
- ✅ Add a **hero image** (a nice photo of their shop or product)

That's it. No writing code, no complicated setup — those four things make a convincing demo.

**3. (Bonus) Pre-qualify the business**
This is where you can earn extra. Call the business and let them know we're coming by, using a short script like:

> *"Hi! We built a free demo app for [Business Name] and we'd love to swing by later today to show you — it only takes 1–2 minutes. Would that be okay?"*

If they say yes, mark it as **pre-qualified** and pass it to the sales team. A business that's expecting us is far easier to close — so every pre-qualified demo earns you a bonus.
