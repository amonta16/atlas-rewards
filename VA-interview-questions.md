# VA Interview Questions — Demo App Builder / Lead Gen

A short guide for interviewing candidates for the role (finding local CA businesses, building demo apps in our builder, and pre-qualifying via a quick call). Works for a live call or async written responses.

**How to use it:** You don't need every question — pick ~8–10. The two parts that matter most are the **phone role-play** and the **practical mini-task** at the end; everything else is supporting. Quick scoring scale at the bottom.

---

## 1. Warm-up

1. Tell me about yourself in 2–3 sentences — what kind of work have you been doing?
2. What made you apply to this role specifically?

---

## 2. Reliability & work style (remote)

3. Walk me through a typical workday for you. How do you stay organized when you're doing the same task many times?
4. This work is repetitive — building lots of similar demos. How do you keep quality high and not rush?
5. Tell me about a time you committed to a deadline or daily target. Did you hit it? What got in the way?
6. How do you prefer to get feedback, and how do you handle being told a demo needs to be redone?

> **Listen for:** specific systems (checklists, trackers), ownership, no excuses, comfort with repetition.

---

## 3. Tech & tools comfort

7. How comfortable are you learning a new web tool from a short walkthrough? Give an example of a tool you picked up quickly.
8. How do you research businesses on Google / Google Maps? What info would you pull for a local coffee shop?
9. If you needed a business's logo and a good photo of their shop, where would you look?

> **Listen for:** resourcefulness, not intimidated by software, knows Google Maps / business listings / websites / social pages.

---

## 4. Attention to detail & taste

10. When you look at a brand, how do you figure out its colors? (No design degree needed — just a sense for matching.)
11. What makes a rewards list feel good for a coffee shop vs. a salon? Give me a couple of example rewards for each.
12. (Show them 2 versions of a demo — one on-brand, one off.) Which is better and why?

> **Listen for:** a decent eye, can match brand colors, common-sense reward ideas, notices sloppy vs. clean.

---

## 5. Phone & outreach — the bonus 🎯

13. How comfortable are you calling a business you've never spoken to? On a scale of 1–10, and why?
14. Tell me about any phone, customer service, or appointment-setting experience you've had.
15. **Role-play:** I'm the owner of a busy cafe and I just picked up the phone. Go — using something like our script: *"Hi! We built a free demo app for your cafe and we'd love to swing by later today to show you — it only takes 1–2 minutes."*
16. (After the role-play) Owner says *"I'm not interested."* — what do you say next?

> **Listen for:** friendly + confident tone, doesn't sound robotic, stays polite under a "no," gets the "yes you can come by" without being pushy.

---

## 6. Research & initiative

17. If I asked you to find 20 good businesses for this in California, how would you decide which ones make the list?
18. What types of businesses do you think would be the easiest "yes" for a loyalty app, and why?

> **Listen for:** targets the right businesses (repeat-visit, local, owner-operated), thinks about fit not just volume.

---

## 7. Logistics

19. What hours per week can you commit, and what's your time zone / overlap with US Pacific time?
20. What's your expected hourly rate? Is a per-lead bonus motivating for you?
21. What's your computer + internet setup? Any issues making calls to US numbers?

---

## 8. Situational / red-flag checks

22. You build 10 demos and realize the colors are wrong on all of them. What do you do?
23. A business says yes to a visit but later that day you realize you got their address wrong. How do you handle it?

> **Listen for:** honesty, fixes their own mistakes, communicates early instead of hiding problems.

---

## 9. Practical mini-task (the real test)

Give a short **paid** trial task — this tells you more than any answer:

- **Option A (build):** "Here's access + a 3-min walkthrough. Build 1 demo for this business: add the logo, colors, 4 rewards, and a hero image. Send it back." → Judges: follows instructions, taste, speed, did they actually nail all 4 items.
- **Option B (outreach):** "Find 5 qualifying CA businesses with name, phone, logo source, and a photo link. Then record a 30-second voice note of your pitch as if calling one." → Judges: research quality + phone presence.

Best candidates do both well.

| Area | Score |
|---|---|
| Reliability / follows process | |
| Tech comfort (learns the builder) | |
| Detail + design eye | |

**Green flags:** organized, asks good questions, clean trial work, warm on the phone, owns mistakes.
**Red flags:** vague answers, sloppy trial demo, robotic/pushy on calls, dodges the rate/availability questions, didn't follow the application instructions.
