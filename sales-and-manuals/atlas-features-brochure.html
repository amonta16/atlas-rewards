03 · Streaks
The habit loop the big chains live on

Turn visits into a
streak they protect.

A check-in streak gives customers a reason to come back tomorrow, not "sometime." Milestones along the path drop real rewards, so the streak is a visible ladder of free stuff — and breaking it hurts. That's exactly why Snapchat and Duolingo built their entire products on it.

  • 🔥
    Milestone rewards

    "Day 5 = Free Latte," "Day 10 = Free Lunch." The product photo sits right on the streak path.

  • ⏰
    Streak-break push

    The day a streak is about to lapse, Atlas pings them: "Don't lose your 9-day streak." Loss aversion does the rest.

  • 🏅
    "X to go" everywhere

    The Home teaser and the full grid both show how close the next reward is, so the goal is always one tap away.

Why it works
Habit

Streaks manufacture visit frequency — the single biggest driver of food & beverage revenue. More visits per member, on autopilot.
