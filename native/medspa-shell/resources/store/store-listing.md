## App name (30 max)
MedSpa App

## Short description (80 max)
Earn points, unlock rewards and manage your membership at your med spa.

## Full description (4,000 max)
MedSpa App is the rewards and membership app for your med spa.

Pick your spa (search by name or scan the QR code at the front desk) and everything from that spa is right there in one place.

What you can do:
• Earn points every visit and see exactly how close you are to your next reward
• Unwrap welcome gifts and special offers from your spa
• Join a membership and manage it right in the app
• Shop your spa's treatments and products, with pay-over-time options at checkout where your spa offers them
• Refer a friend and earn bonus points
• Get a notification when a reward unlocks, an offer drops or it's time to book your next visit
• Book your next visit online, by text or with one tap to call

Your spa, your app. When you pick your spa, the app shows their name, their rewards and their offers.

MedSpa App works with participating med spas. If your spa isn't listed yet, ask them about it.

Questions? Email hello@atlas-engine.app.
